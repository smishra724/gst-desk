Privacy policy

GST Desk Chrome extension and website · last updated 24 October 2026

Summary

GST Desk keeps your data on your own computer. The developer does not collect, receive, sell or share any client data, logins, documents or accounting data.

What the extension stores, and where

  • Client details you enter (names, GSTIN, PAN, addresses, promoters, HSN/SAC codes), documents you upload, download history and settings are saved only in Chrome's local extension storage on your computer.
  • GST portal passwords are encrypted with AES-256 using a key derived from your master password. The master password is never stored; the unlocked key is kept only in memory until Chrome closes.
  • Backups are created only when you click Export, and are saved where you choose.

Where data is sent

  • GST portal (*.gst.gov.in): only when you use the fill or download features, the extension types your saved details into the portal pages you have open, exactly as you would.
  • TallyPrime on your own computer (localhost): the extension reads vouchers and masters from Tally's local port. Nothing leaves your computer.
  • GST Desk website (smishra724.github.io): the extension checks the latest version number, and – on the dashboard page only – shows a read-only summary (client names, GSTINs, download status) to you in your own browser. Passwords and documents are never shared with the page.

Website sign-in

The website's sign-in uses Google Firebase Authentication (email and password) when enabled, or a built-in owner login. Firebase stores your email address to let you sign in; see Google's privacy policy. No client data is stored on the website.

Permissions used

  • storage, unlimitedStorage – save clients and documents on your computer.
  • downloads – name downloaded returns into client folders.
  • Access to *.gst.gov.in – fill forms and download returns when you ask.
  • Access to localhost – read data from TallyPrime on your computer.
  • Access to smishra724.github.io – update check and dashboard link.
